# Ethera: The Stonewrought — concept

**Author and creative direction:** Babuni.dev  
**Edition:** Curated public documentation, prepared 2026-10-28.

Ethera is the current Grimnir chapter set in the fantasy world of Morkahja. Its authored design follows continuing character lives, knowledge, relationships and consequences.

The first playable scope describes Khar-Morr shaping a stone club, handling equipment and transferring a knowledge Shard to the Memory Fountain. A later Need and Divine Orientation milestone is specified in the original design; this public edition does not assert that milestone is implemented.

The public edition adds no game rules, lore or approved future scope. Babuni.dev’s original specifications remain authoritative.
